How members benefit

Every Exponanta session is built around four mechanical conditions that make referrals actually happen.

Vertical community — your industry, your room

Every chapter is vertical-specific. HealthTech founders sit alongside hospital operators and health investors. No generic rooms — only people who understand your market and can make meaningful introductions.

Pitch slot — sharpen your story, get warm intros

6 minutes to present, 14 minutes of structured feedback from investors and operators in the room. Not applause — real critique that sharpens your pitch and surfaces investor interest on the spot.

Matched 1:1 meetings — no ambient networking

Four matched 13-minute 1:1 slots per session, scheduled by the platform based on your stated needs. Not ambient hallway conversation — named, timed, and purposeful introductions.

Category seat — exclusive to your niche

Each chapter has one seat per business category. Once you hold the HealthTech legal seat, the next healthcare lawyer goes on a waitlist. All referrals in your category come directly to you.

Challenge brief — operators meet their vendors

Corporate operators present a real business problem in 6 minutes. The room responds for 14 minutes. Founders and service providers get a live procurement conversation without a cold email.

Online sessions — global reach, zero travel

Quarterly sessions run entirely online. A Boston HealthTech founder can sit in the same room as a Chicago hospital VP and a San Francisco health investor — without a flight or a conference badge.
