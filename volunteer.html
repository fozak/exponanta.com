Why volunteer in your chapter

Members who hold roles are referred more. The room sees their reliability, their commitment, and their work — and refers accordingly.

Maximum visibility

Role-holders are seen every session — by every member, every guest, and every chapter leader. Visibility is the precondition for referrals.

Trust by demonstration

Reliability in a role signals reliability in business. Members who show up, do the work, and follow through receive more confident introductions.

Leadership pathway

Chapter leadership roles are the entry point to co-hosting chapters, launching new verticals, and building influence across the Exponanta network.

Session Operations

Online sessions require dedicated roles to run smoothly. These are the people who make every session professional, on-time, and productive.

Session Host / Facilitator

Runs the session from start to finish. Manages the meeting room, controls timing, admits guests, introduces the spotlight presenter, and keeps the format on track.

High visibility · Every session

Breakout Room Coordinator

Sets up and assigns matched 1:1 breakout rooms for each session. Ensures every member is paired correctly based on platform-matched looking-for declarations.

Technical · Pre-session prep

Recap Coordinator

Produces and sends the session recap within 24 hours — all looking-for declarations, intro cards offered in the referral round, and follow-up notes. The chapter's institutional memory.

Post-session · Writing

Membership & Governance

These roles control quality and growth. Decision-making power sits here — and so does the most visible leadership in the chapter.

Membership Committee

Approves and reviews member applications. Manages category seat conflicts. Handles member issues and renewal decisions. The guardian of chapter quality.

Governance · Decision-making

Scorecard Manager

Maintains member scorecards — sessions attended, referrals given and received, TYFIs logged. Flags attendance gaps and reports activity levels to the Membership Committee.

Accountability · Analytics

Onboarding Coordinator

Guides new Guests through the platform before their first session. Ensures profiles are complete, explains the session format, and answers questions before the first 60-second intro.

Quality · First impressions

Platform & Community

The chapter lives between sessions too. These roles keep the community active, the platform current, and the referral relationships compounding every day — not just every two weeks.

Platform Moderator

Manages the community discussion board between sessions. Approves posts, surfaces relevant discussions, flags issues, and keeps the async layer of the chapter engaged and professional.

Community · Daily

Directory Curator

Keeps member profiles current. Prompts members to update their looking-for declarations before each session. Ensures the directory is useful for cross-chapter referrals.

Platform · Pre-session

Referral Tracker

Logs intro cards and TYFIs in the platform after each session. Follows up on open referrals. Ensures the accountability loop closes — that offered introductions actually happen.

Accountability · Post-session

Support & Experience

Easier entry points for newer members. These roles build your presence in the chapter while contributing directly to member experience.

Visitor Host

Welcomes Guests at the start of each session. Explains the format, introduces them to members, and ensures their first experience is warm, clear, and worth returning for.

Entry level · Guest-facing

Education Coordinator

Shares a short learning tip at each session — best practices for looking-for declarations, spotlight preparation, or referral-giving. Keeps skill development embedded in the format.

Entry level · Prepared content

Mentor Coordinator

Pairs experienced members with new members joining the chapter. Ensures new members understand the format, submit strong looking-for declarations, and get their first 1:1 meetings set up.

Entry level · Relationship-building

Marketing & LinkedIn Growth

Chapter growth depends on visibility. These roles build the chapter's presence on LinkedIn — the primary professional network where Exponanta members live.

LinkedIn Chapter Manager

Owns the chapter's LinkedIn presence. Posts session announcements, member spotlights, referral success stories, and chapter milestones. Grows follower count and engagement consistently.

LinkedIn · Weekly cadence

Content Creator

Writes LinkedIn posts, short-form articles, and member quotes that communicate the value of referral networking. Turns session moments — a strong looking-for declaration, a closed referral — into shareable content.

LinkedIn · Content writing

Member Amplification Lead

Coordinates member LinkedIn activity — encouraging members to share session recaps, tag each other in referral acknowledgements, and post their looking-for declarations publicly to attract inbound referrals from outside the chapter.

LinkedIn · Member coordination

Visitor Day Organizer

Plans and promotes open visitor sessions — LinkedIn campaigns, direct outreach to prospective members, and coordination with the Visitor Host to ensure high-quality Guest attendance.

Growth · Outreach

Analytics & Growth Tracker

Tracks LinkedIn follower growth, post engagement, Guest conversion rates, and referral attribution from social channels. Reports monthly to chapter leadership on what's working.

Data · Monthly reporting

Visual Content Producer

Creates graphics, quote cards, and visual assets for LinkedIn posts — member spotlights, session stat cards, chapter milestones. Ensures the chapter's visual presence is consistent and professional.

Design · LinkedIn assets
