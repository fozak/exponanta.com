What's included in Starter

Starter is not a trial — it is a structured onboarding experience. You get a real prep call, a real pitch slot in front of a live room, and real feedback. The $40 is not a deposit; it is credited in full toward your Member subscription if you join within 12 months.

Structured prep session — before your pitch

A 30-minute call with the session host before your pitch slot. You work through your looking-for declaration, sharpen your 6-minute presentation, and understand what the room will ask. Members who skip prep get weaker feedback.

One pitch slot — 6 min presentation, 14 min feedback

You present for 6 minutes to a live room of investors, operators, and service providers. The room then gives structured feedback for 14 minutes. Not applause — real critique that sharpens your pitch and surfaces warm introductions on the spot.

Live feedback from investors and operators

The feedback panel includes whoever holds category seats in the room — typically VCs, corporate innovation leads, and senior operators in your vertical. One session of this replaces months of cold outreach for feedback that matters.

$40 credited toward Member — never expires

Your $40 Starter fee is credited in full when you subscribe as a Member ($120 for 6 months). You pay $80 to activate. The credit never expires — use it when you are ready. There is no pressure to upgrade immediately after the pitch session.

Everything in Guest — included

Starter includes the two free guest visits. If you have already attended as a Guest, your Starter begins with the prep session and pitch slot — no need to re-attend as an observer.

The value at $40

One hour of structured feedback from a VC or corporate innovation lead typically costs $200–500 as a coaching session. A conference badge where you might speak to an investor costs $400–2,000. Starter delivers a structured prep call, a live pitch slot, and 14 minutes of real feedback for $40 — and the $40 counts toward membership.
