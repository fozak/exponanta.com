Exponanta is not a conference, a directory, or a casual meetup. It is a structured online referral networking community organized into vertical chapters — each running a fixed, proven format twice a month.

Online-first chapters

Every Exponanta chapter runs online. No geography constraints — join the vertical that matches your industry, not just your city.

Referral-first format

Every session has one purpose: warm introductions. The format — 60-second intros, matched 1:1s, spotlight presentations, referral round — is designed to produce referrals, not just goodwill.

Vertical chapters

Industry-specific chapters mean every introduction is relevant. HealthTech, FinTech, EdTech, and more — your chapter is populated with the people who understand your market.

Recurring cadence

Twice a month, every month. The compound effect of consistent attendance — not a one-off event — is what builds the referral relationships that generate real business.

The organization behind Exponanta

The Center for Entrepreneurship (CFE) was established in 2002 with support from the TUSR Investment Fund and USAID. Over 25 years it delivered more than 8,450 events attended by 354,000+ people across 244+ cities and 160+ countries.

CFE's flagship programs — ScaleUp, FastTrac, Startup Club, Startup Huddle, and the Dynamic Entrepreneurship Classroom — produced measurable outcomes: 53% average revenue growth among ScaleUp cohorts, 530+ jobs created, and 1,890+ structured sessions delivered.

Exponanta is the digital evolution of that methodology — taking CFE's structured, outcome-driven program design and applying it to online referral networking chapters.

What your membership delivers

Every benefit exists for one reason: to produce warm introductions.
Named, specific referrals from people accountable to the room.

12 sessions

Twice a month for six months. The cadence burns your looking-for declaration into the room's memory until members refer you automatically — without being asked.

60-second intro

One specific looking-for declaration, stated publicly every session. The room carries your ask between sessions. When the right person appears in their world, they think of you.

48 matched 1:1s

Four 13-minute meetings per session, platform-matched by looking-for declaration. Not random conversation — targeted introductions between people whose stated needs align.

2 spotlight slots

Train the room to describe your business in two sentences — which is exactly what a warm introduction requires. Pitch slot, challenge brief, or case study.

Category seat

One seat per profession per chapter. Every referral the chapter generates for your category routes exclusively to you — not split across three competitors in the same room.

Session recap

Every member's looking-for declaration, every intro card from the referral round, sent within 24 hours. Twelve recaps — a complete record of the chapter's referral activity.
