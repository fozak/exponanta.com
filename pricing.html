Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer? Reach out to us.

How do I attend as a Guest?
Browse the chapter listing, find your vertical, and register for the next session. No application required for your first two guest visits.
Do I need revenue or traction to join?
No. You need a concrete, specific looking-for declaration — not revenue. The more specific your ask, the more valuable you are to the room.
What professions benefit most?
Any profession that grows through introductions. Lawyers, accountants, consultants, recruiters, business owners, executives, and investors all hold category seats that route every relevant referral directly to them.
Are members required to pass referrals?
No quota. The referral round and TYFI (Thank You for the Intro) create a social accountability loop. Members who give generously receive more in return.
How is Exponanta different from a conference?
Conferences broadcast. Exponanta sessions are structured — every member has declared their looking-for declaration, every 1:1 is matched, every intro in the referral round is named and specific. Value compounds across bi-weekly sessions.
What is a Partner?
A company-level membership — typically a VC firm, accelerator, or corporate innovation team — that co-hosts a vertical chapter with 2 seats, 4 spotlight slots, 1 co-hosted session, and branding in all recaps.
