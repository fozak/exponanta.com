Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer? Reach out to us.

How do I attend as a Guest?
Browse the chapter listing, find your vertical, and register for the next session. No application required for your first two guest visits — just show up.
What kind of businesses benefit most?
Any business that grows through relationships. Business owners, independent professionals, and service providers — lawyers, accountants, consultants, recruiters — all hold category seats that route every relevant referral directly to them.
Do I need an established business to join?
You need a specific, concrete looking-for declaration — not revenue or traction. The more specific your ask, the more valuable you are to the room. If you can state clearly who you need to meet and why, you are ready.
Are members required to pass referrals?
No quota. The referral round and TYFI (Thank You for the Intro) create a social accountability loop. Members who give generously receive more in return — the room notices who participates and who does not.
How is Exponanta different from a networking event?
Networking events are unstructured. Exponanta sessions have a fixed format — every member declares their looking-for, every 1:1 is matched, every intro in the referral round is named and specific. Value compounds across bi-weekly sessions rather than resetting each time.
What is a Partner membership?
A company-level membership — typically a professional services firm, accelerator, or corporate team — that co-hosts a vertical chapter with 2 seats, 4 spotlight slots, 1 co-hosted session, and branding in all session recaps.
